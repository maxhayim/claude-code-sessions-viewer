# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience
level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an
issue or contacting the maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated.
